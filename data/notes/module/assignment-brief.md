# Assignment brief summary

Module 55-710603, group project, 60 percent of the module mark. My paraphrase of the
brief; the PDF on Blackboard is authoritative if these disagree.

## Deliverables

- Written report, 6000 words plus or minus 10 percent, excluding references and
  appendices. Harvard referencing.
- Working code with a README that lets the marker run it without asking us anything.
- Fifteen minute group presentation followed by five minutes of questions.
- Individual reflective statement, 500 words, submitted separately.

Weighting inside the 60: report 60 percent, artefact 25 percent, presentation 15 percent.

Report structure the brief asks for: introduction and research question, literature
review, methodology, implementation, evaluation, discussion, conclusion and future work.
Evaluation has to include quantitative results, which is why the gold set exists at all.

## Dates

Ethics form before any data collection. Interim submission in week 7, code plus a
one-page progress note, formative only. Final submission end of week 12, 15:00, through
the Blackboard Turnitin link. Presentations in the week after.

Late penalties are the standard university ones: capped at the pass mark inside 24 hours,
zero after that, and the extenuating circumstances route needs evidence.

Notes to the group from our first meeting: the marking scheme rewards critical evaluation
far more than it rewards features, so a smaller system that we have measured properly
beats a bigger one we have only demonstrated. Two research questions is enough. Anything
we cannot evaluate in the time we have should go in future work rather than into the code.
